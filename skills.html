Decision Making Skills

A decision matrix is important in the engineering process as it helps us make design decisions based on criteria such as consumer needs and design limitations. The design of a decision matrix involves creating a table that lists the design options and the criteria that will be used to evaluate them. Each criterion is assigned a weight based on its importance, and each design option is scored based on how well it meets each criterion. The scores are then multiplied by the weights and summed to give a total score for each design option. This will help us make a more informed decision on which design is best for the customer.
